01 Why

People want experiences.
Not just another adda.

Kathmandu has great cafés, talented artists, fitness studios and genuinely creative people. Those worlds almost never meet — so most free evenings end up looking like the last one.

Paalo puts them in the same room. What comes out is something you can actually book: an evening where you learn something, make something, meet someone, or spend your time somewhere new.
